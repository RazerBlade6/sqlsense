# Campaign 10: Summer health and beauty

## Overview
Campaign 10 is a Type B campaign that ran from 28 June 2017 to 30 July 2017 (weeks 27 to 31). It was sent to 123 households.

## Objective
Grow sun care and summer health-and-beauty lines.

## Target audience
Households that bought health and beauty in the spring campaign.

## Offer
14 coupons covering 393 products, mainly in Drug GM (health and beauty), Grocery, Pastry. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
