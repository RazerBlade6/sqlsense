# Campaign 11: Back to school lunchbox

## Overview
Campaign 11 is a Type B campaign that ran from 12 July 2017 to 27 August 2017 (weeks 29 to 35). It was sent to 214 households.

## Objective
Capture back-to-school lunchbox and snack spend.

## Target audience
Households with school-age children.

## Offer
13 coupons covering 278 products, mainly in Grocery, Drug GM (health and beauty). Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
