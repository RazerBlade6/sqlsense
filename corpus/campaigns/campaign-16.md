# Campaign 16: Beauty event

## Overview
Campaign 16 is a Type B campaign that ran from 4 October 2017 to 5 November 2017 (weeks 41 to 45). It was sent to 188 households.

## Objective
Launch the expanded cosmetics range.

## Target audience
Households that bought cosmetics or personal care in the last 26 weeks.

## Offer
13 coupons covering 1,025 products, mainly in Cosmetics, Drug GM (health and beauty), Grocery. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
