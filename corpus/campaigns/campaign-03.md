# Campaign 3: Health and grocery pilot

## Overview
Campaign 3 is a Type C campaign that ran from 13 March 2017 to 8 May 2017 (weeks 12 to 20). It was sent to 12 households.

## Objective
Test whether a mixed grocery and health-and-beauty offer lifts cross-department shopping.

## Target audience
A small pilot group of high-frequency shoppers.

## Offer
34 coupons covering 506 products, mainly in Grocery, Drug GM (health and beauty), Cosmetics. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 5% (the Type C target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
