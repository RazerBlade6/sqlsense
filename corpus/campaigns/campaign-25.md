# Campaign 25: Winter pantry (prior year)

## Overview
Campaign 25 is a Type B campaign that ran from 6 December 2016 to 5 February 2017 (started before the data period and ran into week 6). It was sent to 17 households.

## Objective
Grow winter pantry staples over the prior year's holidays.

## Target audience
Households with declining grocery spend.

## Offer
28 coupons covering 535 products, mainly in Grocery. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
