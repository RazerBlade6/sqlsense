# Campaign 7: Personal care

## Overview
Campaign 7 is a Type B campaign that ran from 24 April 2017 to 28 May 2017 (weeks 18 to 22). It was sent to 198 households.

## Objective
Increase personal-care spend per trip.

## Target audience
Households already buying some personal-care products.

## Offer
8 coupons covering 259 products, mainly in Drug GM (health and beauty), Grocery, Cosmetics. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
