# Campaign 8: Summer personalised event

## Overview
Campaign 8 is a Type A campaign that ran from 8 May 2017 to 25 June 2017 (weeks 20 to 26). It was sent to 1,076 households.

## Objective
Increase spend from the most valuable households through coupons chosen from their own purchase history.

## Target audience
The top households by spend over the previous 13 weeks.

## Offer
A pool of 209 coupons covering 17,178 products, mainly in Grocery, Meat, Meat-Pckgd. Each household received 16 coupons from the pool, selected from its own purchase history.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 15% (the Type A target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
