# Campaign 6: Coffee loyalty test

## Overview
Campaign 6 is a Type C campaign that ran from 19 April 2017 to 21 May 2017 (weeks 17 to 21). It was sent to 65 households.

## Objective
Test a single-category coffee offer for repeat purchase.

## Target audience
Regular coffee buyers.

## Offer
1 coupons covering 18 products, mainly in Grocery. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 5% (the Type C target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
