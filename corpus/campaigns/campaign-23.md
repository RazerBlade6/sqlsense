# Campaign 23: New year health

## Overview
Campaign 23 is a Type B campaign that ran from 28 December 2017 to 4 February 2018 (started in week 53 and ran past the end of the data period (week 53)). It was sent to 183 households.

## Objective
Capture new-year health resolutions with nutrition and grocery offers.

## Target audience
Households that bought nutrition products in the last year.

## Offer
18 coupons covering 767 products, mainly in Grocery, Nutrition, Drug GM (health and beauty). Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
