# Campaign 5: Health and beauty spring

## Overview
Campaign 5 is a Type B campaign that ran from 3 April 2017 to 7 May 2017 (weeks 15 to 19). It was sent to 166 households.

## Objective
Win share in health and beauty from drugstore competitors.

## Target audience
Households that buy grocery here but little health and beauty.

## Offer
11 coupons covering 443 products, mainly in Drug GM (health and beauty). Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
