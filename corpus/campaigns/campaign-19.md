# Campaign 19: Cold and flu season

## Overview
Campaign 19 is a Type B campaign that ran from 15 November 2017 to 17 December 2017 (weeks 47 to 51). It was sent to 130 households.

## Objective
Grow cold and flu remedies and winter health and beauty.

## Target audience
Households that bought health and beauty in the last 26 weeks.

## Offer
11 coupons covering 375 products, mainly in Drug GM (health and beauty). Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
