# Campaign 9: Healthy summer

## Overview
Campaign 9 is a Type B campaign that ran from 31 May 2017 to 2 July 2017 (weeks 23 to 27). It was sent to 176 households.

## Objective
Grow nutrition and better-for-you grocery lines.

## Target audience
Households buying nutrition products or fresh produce.

## Offer
18 coupons covering 825 products, mainly in Grocery, Drug GM (health and beauty), Nutrition. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
