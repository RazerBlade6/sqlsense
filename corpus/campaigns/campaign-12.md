# Campaign 12: Summer grilling

## Overview
Campaign 12 is a Type B campaign that ran from 12 July 2017 to 13 August 2017 (weeks 29 to 33). It was sent to 170 households.

## Objective
Grow grilling occasions: packaged meat, sauces and sides.

## Target audience
Households that bought packaged meat at least monthly.

## Offer
15 coupons covering 620 products, mainly in Grocery, Meat-Pckgd, Nutrition. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
