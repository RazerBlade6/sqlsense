# Campaign 18: Holiday personalised event

## Overview
Campaign 18 is a Type A campaign that ran from 30 October 2017 to 24 December 2017 (weeks 45 to 52). It was sent to 1,133 households.

## Objective
Maximise holiday-season spend from high-value households with coupons chosen from their purchase history.

## Target audience
The top households by spend over the previous 13 weeks.

## Offer
A pool of 209 coupons covering 35,513 products, mainly in Drug GM (health and beauty), Grocery, Cosmetics. Each household received 16 coupons from the pool, selected from its own purchase history.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 15% (the Type A target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
