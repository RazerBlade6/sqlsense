# Campaign 20: Winter wellness pilot

## Overview
Campaign 20 is a Type C campaign that ran from 27 November 2017 to 5 February 2018 (started in week 49 and ran past the end of the data period (week 53)). It was sent to 244 households.

## Objective
Test a long-running winter wellness offer spanning grocery and health and beauty.

## Target audience
A pilot group of households with older heads of household.

## Offer
24 coupons covering 609 products, mainly in Drug GM (health and beauty), Grocery. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 5% (the Type C target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
