# Campaign 26: New year pantry

## Overview
Campaign 26 is a Type B campaign that ran from 28 December 2016 to 19 February 2017 (started before the data period and ran into week 8). It was sent to 118 households.

## Objective
Restart grocery spend after the holidays.

## Target audience
Households that shopped less in December than in November.

## Offer
33 coupons covering 689 products, mainly in Grocery, Nutrition, Drug GM (health and beauty). Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
