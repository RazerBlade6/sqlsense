# Campaign 21: Holiday entertaining

## Overview
Campaign 21 is a Type B campaign that ran from 6 December 2017 to 7 January 2018 (started in week 50 and ran past the end of the data period (week 53)). It was sent to 65 households.

## Objective
Grow holiday party spend in deli, seafood and grocery.

## Target audience
Households that bought deli platters or seafood last December.

## Offer
16 coupons covering 242 products, mainly in Grocery, Deli, Seafood-Pckgd. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
