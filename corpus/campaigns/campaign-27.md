# Campaign 27: Winter personalised event

## Overview
Campaign 27 is a Type A campaign that ran from 8 February 2017 to 26 March 2017 (weeks 7 to 13). It was sent to 361 households.

## Objective
Increase late-winter spend from high-value households with coupons chosen from their purchase history.

## Target audience
The top households by spend over the previous 13 weeks.

## Offer
A pool of 181 coupons covering 3,205 products, mainly in Grocery, Drug GM (health and beauty), Meat-Pckgd. Each household received 16 coupons from the pool, selected from its own purchase history.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 15% (the Type A target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
