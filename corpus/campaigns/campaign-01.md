# Campaign 1: Spring pantry restock

## Overview
Campaign 1 is a Type B campaign that ran from 3 March 2017 to 9 April 2017 (weeks 10 to 15). It was sent to 13 households.

## Objective
Bring lapsed pantry shoppers back to centre-store grocery ahead of Easter.

## Target audience
Households whose dry-grocery spend fell over the winter.

## Offer
11 coupons covering 383 products, mainly in Grocery. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
