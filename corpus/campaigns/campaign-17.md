# Campaign 17: Thanksgiving dinner

## Overview
Campaign 17 is a Type B campaign that ran from 18 October 2017 to 19 November 2017 (weeks 43 to 47). It was sent to 202 households.

## Objective
Win the Thanksgiving meal: grocery staples, packaged seafood and meat.

## Target audience
Households that shopped in Thanksgiving week last year.

## Offer
19 coupons covering 713 products, mainly in Grocery, Seafood-Pckgd, Meat-Pckgd. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
