# Campaign 14: Fall cross-department pilot

## Overview
Campaign 14 is a Type C campaign that ran from 4 September 2017 to 8 November 2017 (weeks 37 to 46). It was sent to 224 households.

## Objective
Test a long-running grocery and health-and-beauty offer for basket building.

## Target audience
A pilot group of mid-spend households.

## Offer
26 coupons covering 714 products, mainly in Grocery, Drug GM (health and beauty), Cosmetics. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 5% (the Type C target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
