# Campaign 24: Holiday pilot (prior year)

## Overview
Campaign 24 is a Type C campaign that ran from 14 November 2016 to 16 January 2017 (started before the data period and ran into week 4). It was sent to 12 households.

## Objective
Test a mixed grocery and health-and-beauty offer over the prior year's holidays.

## Target audience
A small pilot group.

## Offer
27 coupons covering 632 products, mainly in Grocery, Drug GM (health and beauty). Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 5% (the Type C target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
