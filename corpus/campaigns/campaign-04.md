# Campaign 4: Snacks and beverages

## Overview
Campaign 4 is a Type B campaign that ran from 29 March 2017 to 30 April 2017 (weeks 14 to 18). It was sent to 81 households.

## Objective
Grow snack and beverage volume ahead of the spring holidays.

## Target audience
Households with young children that buy snacks regularly.

## Offer
12 coupons covering 200 products, mainly in Grocery, Drug GM (health and beauty). Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
