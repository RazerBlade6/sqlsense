# Campaign 15: Holiday baking test

## Overview
Campaign 15 is a Type C campaign that ran from 20 September 2017 to 28 February 2018 (started in week 39 and ran past the end of the data period (week 53)). It was sent to 17 households.

## Objective
Test a narrow baking offer across the holiday season.

## Target audience
A small group of households that bake regularly.

## Offer
2 coupons covering 55 products, mainly in Grocery. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 5% (the Type C target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
