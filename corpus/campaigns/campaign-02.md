# Campaign 2: Easter baking and meat

## Overview
Campaign 2 is a Type B campaign that ran from 8 March 2017 to 9 April 2017 (weeks 11 to 15). It was sent to 48 households.

## Objective
Grow Easter-week baskets by pairing baking staples with packaged meat.

## Target audience
Households that bought baking ingredients in the previous Easter season.

## Offer
16 coupons covering 301 products, mainly in Grocery, Meat-Pckgd. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
