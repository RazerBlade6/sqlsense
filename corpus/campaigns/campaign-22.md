# Campaign 22: Holiday gifting and health and beauty

## Overview
Campaign 22 is a Type B campaign that ran from 6 December 2017 to 7 January 2018 (started in week 50 and ran past the end of the data period (week 53)). It was sent to 276 households.

## Objective
Grow gift-able health and beauty and grocery lines in December.

## Target audience
Households active in the beauty event.

## Offer
21 coupons covering 1,917 products, mainly in Drug GM (health and beauty), Grocery, Meat-Pckgd. Every household in the campaign received every coupon.

## Success measures
- **Primary:** coupon redemption rate of at least 8% (the Type B target). See [Coupon redemption rate](../metrics/coupon-redemption-rate.md).
- **Secondary:** spend per participating household during the campaign weeks, compared with the same number of weeks before it.
